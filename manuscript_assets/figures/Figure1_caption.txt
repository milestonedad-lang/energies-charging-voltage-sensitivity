Figure 1 Paired voltage decisions across alternative mappings. Panels a and b show the 33-bus and 69-bus feeders, respectively. Each point is the percentage of 4,344 paired hours with different violation decisions between A and the indicated energy-matched input. All 20 mappings per feeder are shown; mapping 0 is excluded. The voltage limits are 0.95 and 1.05 p.u.; other settings match the main analysis. Mappings are synthetic scenarios, not independent empirical replicates. Small horizontal offsets distinguish inputs, and no lines connect the unordered mapping IDs. Complete values are in Supplementary Table S5 and the figure source data.
